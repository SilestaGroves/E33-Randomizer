## Tracker hotkeys and mini mode

- **Hotkeys that work in the game:** **Ctrl+Shift+T** shows or hides the tracker, **Ctrl+Shift+M** switches between the full tracker and the mini mode. Both can be changed in the **Misc** tab (click the field and press the new combination). They work while the randomizer is running, also while the game has the focus.
- **Mini mode:** a small panel in a corner of the screen (top right by default, the corner is chosen in the Misc tab) with the current region, what's left to find there, and the key item count. It never takes the focus from the game, never brings up the cursor, and mouse clicks go through it. Open it with the hotkey or with **Mini mode** at the bottom of the tracker.
- **Follows you:** after each save the tracker switches to the region the game was saved in, so the mini panel shows where you are. Picking another region yourself sticks until you save somewhere else.
- The tracker settings (on/off, hotkeys, corner) are now kept between runs.

Over the game the tracker shows in windowed and borderless mode; in exclusive fullscreen Windows doesn't draw other windows over the game.
