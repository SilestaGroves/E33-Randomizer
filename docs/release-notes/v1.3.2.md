## Replacements are as strong as the enemies they replace

Enemy stats come from the enemy's archetype (regular, strong, elite, boss, chromatic...) and the fight's level. Before, a regular boss in place of a chromatic one was far weaker even at the same level.

- New option, on by default: "Replacements take the strength of the enemy they replace". A replacement gets the archetype of the enemy it replaces: a chromatic in place of a chromatic, an elite in place of an elite. Its model, attacks and loot stay its own.
- With "Scale enemy levels to the encounter", this makes a replacement about as strong as the original enemy on every difficulty.

## Tutorial fights are randomized

Only the scripted story battles (Gustave vs Renoir, the final duel) keep their enemies. The option is now called "Keep scripted story battles".

## Game update: anniversary haircuts

The randomizer's game files are updated to the anniversary update (Steam build 23765773). Before, "Make every item visible" put the older item tables back, which removed the six new anniversary haircuts from the game. They are now cosmetics like the others.

## The mod is checked against the installed game

When the game folder is set, the files the mod replaces are compared with the installed game. If the game was updated after this version of the randomizer, the result message warns about it, and generation_log.txt lists the files.

Generate the mod again with this version.

Updating: older versions offer this update on startup.
