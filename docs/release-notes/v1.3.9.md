## Skills drop from enemies (experimental)

A new option on the Skill Randomizer tab: **Skills drop from enemies (learned the moment they drop)**.

- Every skill of the randomized trees becomes an item in the loot of two enemies that are fought in this seed. The item always drops, and the moment it does, the skill is unlocked and learned: no skill points needed. Until then the skill is hidden in the tree.
- Skills a character has from the start stay, and so does Gustave's Overcharge when "Keep Gustave's Overcharge" is on.
- Only enemies you can farm carry skills: fights in areas that can be revisited, not the finale, the DLC or scripted story battles, and no tutorial versions, summons or clones. Enemies met in several fights are preferred.
- The spoiler log has a new section, **SKILLS DROPPED BY ENEMIES**, listing the enemies for every skill.
- Works with and without the item and enemy randomizers.

The approach (a skill unlock item per skill, free starting hidden nodes) comes from Ihor Chornyi's E33 Randomizer (MIT license). It's off by default; please report how it behaves in the game.
