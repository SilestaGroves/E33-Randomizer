## Dark theme

A "Dark theme" checkbox at the bottom of the main window switches all windows to a dark look at once. The choice is remembered on this computer (theme.txt next to the exe).

- Graphite backgrounds with light grey text instead of white on black.
- Muted versions of the light theme's colours: teal instead of bright cyan buttons, a soft green "Generate" button, amber hints, a muted red for "-" buttons.
- Checkboxes and sliders use your Windows accent colour.
- The light theme looks as before.
