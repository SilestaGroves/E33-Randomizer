## Random number of bosses in boss fights

A new option on the Enemy Randomizer tab: **Random number of bosses in boss fights, between N and M** (1 to 3 by default).

- Every fight that has a boss or a chromatic boss gets that many bosses; the fight's other enemies stay. Regular fights aren't affected (their size still follows "Randomize encounter sizes").
- The boss slots are filled by the placement rules, and when a rule gives a non-boss there, a random boss takes its place, so the fight really has that many bosses.
- With "Replacements take the strength of the enemy they replace", every added boss gets the strength of the fight's own boss, not of its minions.
- At most one giant per fight, and at most 4 enemies, since the game only places 4 in the arena. Scripted story battles, fights protected by other options, and enemies a boss summons or clones mid-fight are left as they are.
