## Russian interface, tooltips and mod removal

- **Language:** a "Language" list at the bottom of the main window switches every window between English and Russian at once. The first start follows the Windows language; the choice is remembered on this computer (language.txt next to the exe). Enemy, item and skill names stay as in the game data.
- **Tooltips:** every setting and button now explains what it does when you hover over it.
- **Remove mod from game:** a new button next to the game folder deletes the randomizer's files from the game's ~mods folder (after asking). Other mods and saves aren't touched.

### Fixes
- Dark theme: the enemy tab's "Customize Enemy Placements" and "Edit Individual Encounters" buttons were pushed out of the window by the theme's taller check boxes. Check boxes are compact again, and the tabs scroll when their content doesn't fit.
- The item tab's last button was cut off at the minimum window size; the tab now scrolls.
- A long warning on the enemy tab was cut off instead of wrapping.
