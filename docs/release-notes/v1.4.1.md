## Damage cap pictos only in Act III

A new option on the Item Randomizer tab, on by default: **Other damage cap pictos only in Act III**.

- Only two pictos let damage go past 9999: **Painted Power**, and the cut **BlankPictos**, which has exactly the same effect (checked in the game files: they're the only two that use the "Damage can exceed 9,999" passive).
- Painted Power's original copy, the cutscene at the start of Act III, always stays.
- Every other copy of either that the item randomizer placed moves to an Act III place that can be revisited (not Lumière in Act III, which can't be revisited); the places they leave get ordinary items.
