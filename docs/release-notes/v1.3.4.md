## Fix: wrong enemy levels (the level scaling option is removed)

The game already gives every fight the right level. A fight without a level of its own is fought at the level of the map it is on: 3 in Spring Meadows, 8 in Flying Waters, 30 in the Abbest cave, and so on. The enemy's own level doesn't matter there.

The "Scale enemy levels to the encounter" option wrote the enemy's own level into fights instead. In Spring Meadows, the Mime was fought at level 13 instead of 3. A replacement for the Abbest cave's chromatic boss was fought at level 5 instead of 30.

- The option is removed. Fights keep their original levels, so any replacement is fought at the level of the place it stands in.
- Together with "Replacements take the strength of the enemy they replace", a replacement is about as strong as the enemy it replaces.
- The spoiler log shows "fought at the area's level" for these fights, and the level for fights that have one of their own.

Generate the mod again with this version.

Updating: older versions offer this update on startup.
