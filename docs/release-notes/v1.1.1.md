## Download

- **E33Randomizer-v1.1.1-win-x64.zip** — unpack anywhere (not an admin folder) and run `E33Randomizer.exe`. Nothing else to install.
- **E33Randomizer-v1.1.1-win-x64-framework.zip** — a much smaller download for those who have the [.NET 9 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/9.0) installed.

## Fix: crash after the Gommage

The Gommage cutscene gives Gustave his expedition outfit, takes away his Lumière suit and puts the expedition outfit on him. The randomizer replaced the outfit that is given, so the game tried to dress Gustave in an outfit he didn't have and crashed right after the cutscene.

Cosmetics that the game also puts on a character are no longer randomized. Besides the Gommage this covers Maelle's "Real Me" outfit and haircut in Act III, Amandine's haircut for Gustave in the prologue and Grandis's French bob for Maelle.

Generate the mod again with this version and replace the old one in the game (it's copied automatically if the game folder is set). If your save is from before the Gommage, just load it. The same seed gives a slightly different result than in v1.1.0.
